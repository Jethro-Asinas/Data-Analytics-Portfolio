# Sample Campaign Materials

These sample campaign graphics show how the team translated the audience research into youth-oriented messaging about education, civic participation, and the local impact of voting.

My role as the team’s data analyst was to analyze demographic and occupation data, develop the audience profile, and provide recommendations that informed the campaign’s themes and messaging. The graphics were produced by members of the design team with assistance from me in a supporting design role.
